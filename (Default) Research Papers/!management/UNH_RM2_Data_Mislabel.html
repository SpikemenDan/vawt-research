The "UNH-RVAT" data in our notebook is actually RM2

The five files in TurbineResearch/unh_data are from the RM2 turbine, but the notebook labels them UNH-RVAT and assigns c/R = 0.28. The correct average is about 0.10.

The measured TSR and Cp values are fine. The geometry attached to them is wrong, so these 125 points sit at 2.8 times their true solidity and Flow Opacity in our plots. Nothing has been changed yet; the fix is on hold until this has been reviewed.

What is wrong

UNH ran two tow-tank studies, published in the same year, at the same five speeds (0.4 to 1.2 m/s), and both name their files Perf-1.0.csv and so on. That is probably how they were confused. They are different rotors.

How we know

The curves peak in the wrong place for RVAT. Real RVAT peaks near TSR 1.9. Every one of our files peaks at TSR 3.1 to 3.4, which is where RM2 operates.

Proposed fix (not applied)

In notebook Block 1, replace the RVAT assumptions and rebuild the two master parquet files:

df_unh_raw['dataset_source'] = 'RM2'
df_unh_raw['cToR'] = 0.10                    # average of a tapered blade
df_unh_raw['Re_D'] = df_unh_raw['mean_tow_speed'] * 1.075 / 1.0e-6

Then add the real RVAT data from its public repository as a separate source, using c/R 0.28 and D = 1 m. Both are already listed as pending in the metadata workbook.
